Shatterfall

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what data the Shatterfall app ("the game") uses. The short version: your game progress stays on your device, we have no accounts and no servers, and the only data that leaves your device is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects to show ads.

1. Your game progress

Your stages, scores, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run any servers and never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch one to continue a stage or to retry the daily challenge. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone) if you allow tracking;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data from the ad software.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices

  • Tracking: on iPhone the game asks for permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EU, UK and similar regions: where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown.
  • No ads: ads only play when you choose to watch one.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the ads, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell personal data.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
